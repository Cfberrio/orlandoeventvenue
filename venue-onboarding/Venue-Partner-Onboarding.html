Welcome to Reliable Venues

We turn busy venues into smooth, modern booking machines. You bring the space and the details — we build you a beautiful website, take bookings and payments online, and handle the back-and-forth automatically so you stop living in spreadsheets and texts.

This packet is how we learn your business. Fill in what you can in plain language — no tech knowledge needed. If something doesn't apply to you, just write "N/A" or leave it blank and we'll sort it out together. Most owners finish in about 20–30 minutes.

Before you start, have these handy

Your logo (an image file, if you have one)
15–25 good photos of your space
Your prices (rental, cleaning, deposit, fees)
Your venue rules for guests
Your hours and any days you're closed
Your social media links
Whoever should be the main point of contact
Your cancellation / refund policy
The fastest way to fill this out: answer the questions you know off the top of your head first, then circle back for the rest. You can email us your logo and photos separately — just check the boxes in Section 2 so we know they're coming.

How to send it back

Type your answers right in this document, or print it and write by hand. Send the completed packet plus your logo and photos to your Reliable Venues contact. We'll review everything within 2 business days and book a 30-minute call to confirm before we start building.

Step 1 of 2 · Pick your package

Which setup is right for you?

Choose the package that fits where your venue is today. You can always upgrade later — many partners start lean and grow into the full platform.

Package A — Full Booking Platform

Most popular
Best if you want guests to book and pay online, and you want one place to run everything.
  • A branded website with your name, colors, photos and pricing
  • Online booking — guests pick a date, customize, sign, and pay a deposit
  • Card payments that land in your account
  • Automatic messages: confirmations, reminders, balance-due, post-event thank-you
  • A private dashboard for your bookings, calendar, staff, cleaning and revenue

Package B — Lead-Capture Landing Page

Best if you just want more inquiries and prefer to handle booking & payment yourself.
  • A beautiful one-page website that shows off your space
  • A contact & "request a tour" form so leads reach you instantly
  • Photo gallery, pricing display, and a welcome-discount popup
  • No online booking calendar or payments — you take it from the inquiry
